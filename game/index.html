EMBERCLASH

Duel of the Lantern Keepers

Two keepers. One lantern. Thirty embers of life each.
Summon guardians of Flame, Tide, Leaf and Stone — and snuff out your rival's light.

How to play

  • Goal: reduce Rook's Ember (life) from 30 to 0 before yours reaches 0.
  • Each turn you gain +1 mana (up to 10) and draw a card. Click a card in your hand to play it — guardians join your side of the table, spells resolve at once.
  • Guardians show attack (bottom-left) and health (bottom-right). When you end your turn, each of your guardians strikes the guardian across from it — or Rook himself if the way is clear.
  • Suits: Flame burns the enemy on summon · Tide heals you · Leaf draws a card · Stone raises a shield that soaks damage.
  • Keyboard: keys 1–7 play a card from your hand · Enter ends your turn · Esc cancels a selection · M mutes sound.

An original game · no network · best with sound on
